# Calendar updates

- **Your calendar now follows your RSVP.** Some calendar invites were being
  ignored by calendar apps, so a change you made on the website (like
  switching to Going) might not show up in your calendar. Invites now always
  update it.
- If your calendar still shows an old answer for one of our upcoming
  {{events}}, change your RSVP on the website and the next invite will fix it.
